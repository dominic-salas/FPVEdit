## Bugs found in the current code (fix list)
- Render on FFmpeg 6.1 uses ~6 GB RAM on a 360p proxy; on FFmpeg 7.0 it silently writes 25 fps.
  Fix: one select-based render chain that works on all versions, plus fps checks in validation.
- Output colour range differs by FFmpeg version (full vs limited). Fix: always write standard limited range.
- When audio starts after video (your files: 0.05-0.10 s), the first segment's audio is ~0.07 s early. Fix: pad with silence.
- Analysis includes the black pillarbox bars and the OSD; both distort the measurements. Fix: crop to the 4:3 picture, mask the OSD automatically.
- Git safety: `res/`, blackbox logs, .srt/.osd files and analysis outputs are not ignored yet. The pre-commit hook is not active on your clone.

# Round 2: adversarial checks (2026-10-11)

## Rotation signal
- **Detects labelled tricks.** Per-sample AUC of 4 s accumulated rotation: 0.82 (182156) and 0.76 (180033), against 0.61 and 0.45 for the M2 motion score. At 60 fps it finds 19/19 individual flips in 182156.
- **Does not predict Dom's own edit.** AUC for kept vs cut in-flight time is 0.55 (clip1) and 0.39 (clip2). Dom cut real flips, for example at clip1 46.0 s and clip2 155.7 s.
- **Fixed thresholds fail under strict scoring.** With 180°/1 s or 360°/4 s and an 80 % coverage rule:
  - 182156: 8/12 events found.
  - 180033: 1/10 events found, and it fires on the ground tap and the dome tumble.
- **Needs every frame.** At 25-30 fps, tracking fails during fast flips: 0-1/19 flips with fixed thresholds, 5-9/19 with per-flight percentiles. The 180033 tricks lose less.
- **Lens field of view not pinned down.** Equidistant HFOV 100-140 fits about equally well.
- **The orbit is not separable** with the rotation magnitude (84th percentile). A 5 s yaw integral reaches the 98.5th percentile, but that is post hoc and based on one event.
