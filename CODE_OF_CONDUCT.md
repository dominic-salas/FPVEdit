# Code of conduct

FPVEdit is a small project for pilots who want to share their flying. Everyone who takes part
in it (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, whatever someone's experience, background or identity;
- give and accept feedback about the work, not the person;
- keep discussions on topic and assume good intent;
- respect privacy: do not share other people's footage, locations or personal details.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexual content, threats and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable anywhere in the project.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@dominic-salas](https://github.com/dominic-salas)). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block people who do not follow this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
